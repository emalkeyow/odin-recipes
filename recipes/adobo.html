Adobo

Description

Pork Adobo (or adobong baboy) is a traditional Filipino dish consisting of pork, typically pork belly or pork shoulder, that is braised in a savory and tangy sauce.

It is widely considered the unofficial national dish of the Philippines, with roots in an indigenous preservation method using vinegar that predates Spanish influence

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs. pork belly
  • 2 tablespoons garlic, crushed and minced
  • 5 pieces dried bay leaves
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon black pepper
  • 1 to 2 cups water
  • salt to taste

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, combine the pork, soy sauce, and garlic, stir until well combined.
  2. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or overnight for best results.
  3. Preheat the pan or cooking pot.
  4. once hot, put the pork mixture into the pan (this includes the soy sauce and garlic).
  5. cook this until the pork is browned
  6. add the black pepper and bay leaves, stir-fry and make sure that all ingredients are well combined.
  7. add water and simmer until the pork is tender.
  8. cover the pot and let it cook on low heat for about 30 minutes or until the sauce has reduced and the pork is fork-tender.
  9. now, add the vinegar and continue cooking for another 10 minutes.
  10. add salt to taste and serve hot with rice.
