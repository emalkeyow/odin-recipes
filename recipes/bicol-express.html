Bicol Express

Description

Bicol Express is a traditional Filipino dish. It is composed of pork that has been simmered in coconut milk. The dish has a creamy, savory, and spicy flavor profile.

It is an excellent dish for dinner parties or any other gathering where you would like to share a meal with friends and family.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. pork belly, cut into chunks
  • 2 cups coconut milk
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp. ginger, minced
  • 2 tbsp. cooking oil
  • 1 tsp. salt
  • 1/2 tsp. black pepper
  • 1 cup water

Steps

  1. Heat the cooking oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add the pork and cook until browned on all sides.
  3. Add the onion, garlic, and ginger. Cook for 2-3 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Pour in the coconut milk and water. Bring to a boil.
  5. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 30-40 minutes or until the pork is tender.
  6. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
  7. Serve hot with rice.
